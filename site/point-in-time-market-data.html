Point-in-time US stock market data for 2020, 2022 and 2026

Three frozen US stock markets ship with the open-source Risk-Aware Trading Agents package: the 2020 COVID crash, the 2022 bear market and 2026. Each has daily prices, the VIX term structure and the macro calendar, every value as it was known at that day's close. Every backtest run on it gives the same numbers on any machine, and the files carry SHA-256 hashes that a paper can cite. The LLM trading leaderboard trades these markets with news replayed by the minute it was published. Its results are based on that news; the headline data has license restrictions and is not distributed with the package.

The two years before each test window are there so that slow indicators such as the 200-day EMA and the 52-week high are fully warmed up on its first trading day. The three markets differ on purpose: a strategy or an LLM that only did well in one kind of market says little.

The news is not part of the data. The leaderboard replays Benzinga headlines (titles only) for the 64 stocks, and in 2026 also SEC 8-K filings with the time SEC accepted them, each from the first close after it was published. Its results are based on that news; the headline data has license restrictions and is not distributed with the package.

  • open to close: prices adjusted for splits only;

Load it

from riskaware.research.data import bundled_market

market = bundled_market("2022")    # "2026" (the default), "2022" or "2020"
aapl = market.frame("AAPL")        # total-return OHLCV, 2020-01-02 to 2022-12-30
traded = market.raw("AAPL")        # split-adjusted prices, dividends, splits, adj factor
vix = market.raw("^VIX")["close"]

import pandas as pd                # or read a file directly
prices = pd.read_csv("src/riskaware/research/data/market_2020.csv.gz", parse_dates=["date"])

One day at a time

The point of the data is what was known on each day. riskaware research day 2026-04-08 (or market_day over MCP) shows everything known at that close: the market regime, VIX and its term structure, fear and greed, sector breadth, the macro events of the next two weeks, and for each stock its returns, indicators and technical setups. Any day of the three test windows works; the command picks the period that holds it.

What "point in time" means here

  • Indicators are computed only from earlier bars; the tests change everything after a day and check that the day's values do not move.
  • News counts from the first close after its publication time (13:00 on early-close days).
  • VIX indices close at 16:15, a quarter of an hour after stocks; decisions are taken after the close, before the next open, when they are known.
  • Futures, the dollar index and bitcoin itself are left out because they settle after the stock close.

Cite it

Cite the repository (its CITATION.cff gives the citation) with the period's data version (2026-11-11, 2022-12-30 or 2020-12-31) and the SHA-256 of its price file from its manifest. Every experiment's own manifest records the package version and commit.

Sources and limits

  • Prices come from Yahoo Finance daily bars; scripts/build_market_bundle.py rebuilds the files. They are adjusted for every split up to the day a file was built (November 2026), so a stock that split later shows smaller prices than were quoted at the time: NVDA's 2020 prices are divided by 40 for its 2021 and 2024 splits. Returns are not affected.
  • The stock lists are today's large caps, chosen with hindsight and used for 2020 and 2022 too (survivorship bias): compare strategies with each other and with the equal-weight universe, not only with SPY.
  • The benchmark's news is one headline feed, plus SEC filings in 2026, so most stories have a single source.
