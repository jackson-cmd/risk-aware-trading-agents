An open-source, long-only system for US stocks. It buys pullbacks to EMA 50/100/200 and quality dips that pass an earnings check, follows trends when the market is healthy, holds instead of panic-selling when it is not, and always keeps 30% in cash or T-bills. Backtest it, paper trade it, then connect one of 10 brokers.开源的美股做多系统。回踩 EMA 50/100/200 时买入，财报过关的好公司大跌时抄底；行情好时顺势持有，行情不明朗时只持有、不恐慌卖出；现金加短期国债始终不低于 30%。先回测，再模拟盘，然后接入 10 家券商中的任意一家。

What it does它能做什么

Every agent writes down why it said yes or no, so each trade comes with its reasons.每个智能体都会写下赞成或反对的理由，所以每笔交易都能看到依据。

Macro-aware regimes看宏观的市场状态

VIX and its term structure, sector breadth, credit appetite, a fear-and-greed gauge and the FOMC, CPI and jobs-report calendar decide one of four regimes.根据 VIX 及其期限结构、板块广度、信用利差、恐慌贪婪指数，以及 FOMC、CPI、非农日历，把市场分成四种状态。

Buy low, sell high低买高卖

Pullbacks to EMA 50/100/200, RSI(2) mean reversion, quality dips after an earnings check, breakouts in strong markets; trims winners when they are stretched.回踩 EMA 50/100/200、RSI(2) 均值回归、财报过关后抄底、强势行情做突破；涨得太远时减仓止盈。

Risk first风控优先

30% cash + T-bill floor, position, sector and speculative caps, volatility-scaled sizes, buying in tranches, Treasury sleeve, drawdown pause, kill switch.现金加短债 30% 底线，单票、板块、高波动仓位上限，按波动率定仓位，分批建仓，国债仓位，回撤暂停，一键停止。

No panic selling不恐慌卖出

In uncertain or risk-off markets the agent holds and only buys at strong support. Only a disaster the company itself confirms (bankruptcy, restatement) can force an exit.不确定或避险时只持有，只在强支撑位买入。只有公司自己确认的重大利空（破产、财报重述）才会强制卖出。

Cross-verified news交叉验证新闻

Finviz, Google News, Nasdaq, Seeking Alpha, Yahoo and SEC 8-K filings. Red flags from two independent outlets block new buys; only the company's own 8-K, or its press release confirming earlier reports, can force a sale, so one fake headline or press release cannot.汇总 Finviz、Google News、Nasdaq、Seeking Alpha、Yahoo 和 SEC 8-K。两家独立媒体确认的风险信号会挡住买入；只有公司自己的 8-K 公告，或者公司通稿证实了先前的报道，才会触发强制卖出，一条假新闻或一份假通稿都带不动它。

Bring your own strategy写你自己的策略

from riskaware.strategy import Strategy, Signal, Action

class Rsi2Dip(Strategy):
    name = "rsi2_dip"
    def on_bar(self, ctx):
        out = []
        for sym in ctx.universe:
            row = ctx.row(sym)
            if row and row["close"] > row["ema200"] and row["rsi2"] < 10:
                out.append(Signal(sym, Action.BUY, strength=0.7, reason="RSI2 dip"))
        return out

FAQ

Will it place real trades by itself?它会自己下实盘单吗？

No. It starts in paper mode. A live order needs execution.mode: live in the config, the --live flag and a confirmation (or --yes). riskaware halt stops all orders.不会。默认是模拟盘。实盘需要配置文件里写 execution.mode: live、命令加 --live，并手动确认（或加 --yes）。riskaware halt 可以一键停止所有下单。

Do I need paid data?需要付费数据吗？

No. Prices come from Yahoo Finance, news from Finviz and public RSS feeds, calendars from the Federal Reserve, BLS and BEA. You can also load your own CSV files.不需要。价格来自 Yahoo Finance，新闻来自 Finviz 和公开 RSS，宏观日历来自美联储、BLS 和 BEA。也可以导入自己的 CSV 数据。

Why not sell when the market is scary?市场吓人的时候为什么不卖？

Panic selling at the bottom is how long-term investors lose most. Losses are bounded up front by diversification, position caps and the cash floor; the agent uses fear to buy quality at support instead.长期投资者最大的损失，往往来自在底部恐慌卖出。这个系统事先用分散持仓、仓位上限和现金底线控制住亏损，市场恐慌时反而在支撑位买入优质股。

Is this investment advice?这是投资建议吗？

No. It is software for research and education. Markets are risky; trade at your own risk.不是。这是用于研究和学习的软件。市场有风险，交易后果自负。
